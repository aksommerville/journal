# Dot Vine Guidelines

Dot is my Mario, she's the star of every game when there isn't a more obvious choice.

## Appearance

- Floppy purple smock and hat.
- Leather belt with a brass buckle, and an identical belt on the hat.
- Two buttons above the belt, of uncertain purpose.
- Tangly candy-apple-red hair.
- Pale skin and freckles.
- Big clunky boots.
- Optional backpack. I've drawn her with it sometimes, and it makes sense, but usually not.

## Rules

- No riding animals.
- No sex appeal.
- If she disperses into Mega Man style circles on death, there must be seven of them.
- - No non-witch character may have more than six circles.
- If making music is a thing, violins are preferred.
- Violence and similar immoral behavior is permitted but keep it mild.
- A general guideline: If you wouldn't show it to your ten-year-old niece, it doesn't belong in a Dot game.

## Errata

- Dot lives in Kansas, in the 13th century. These have both been mentioned obliquely, in Full Moon and Too Heavy.
- Don't lean too hard on the 13th-century thing. Anachronism is allowed and warmly encouraged.
- If we ever do a more formal taxonomy of witchcraft, Dot is a Floramancer: She specializes in plant magic.

## Friends

Put people in a Dot game as needed and characterize them as needed.
The main rule for all characters is they are whatever the game needs them to be.

- Moon Song. Friend and rival. Korean. Wears a pink smock and hat. Full Moon, Spelling Bee, Opener of Cages, Vexularg, Bellacopia.
- Goody Grief. Head of the coven. Saucy in an awkward way, think Nanny Ogg. Full Moon, Too Heavy, Spelling Bee, Bellacopia.
- Princess Priscilla. Dead Weight and Bellacopia. Never said her name in a game (hadn't picked one yet). Blue hair. Helpless, needs protected. Dead Weight, Bellacopia.
- - Queen of Clocks had a Princess, but it wasn't Priscilla. I hadn't settled on her as a permanent character at that time.
- Silver Sal. Swordsman and rogue. Planned as a hero in Fly By Night, but I'm not sure I'm ever making that one.
- Parsley Onion. Archer. '' Fly By Night.
